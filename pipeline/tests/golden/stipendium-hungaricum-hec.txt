The Higher Education Commission (HEC) invites applications from Pakistani/AJ&K nationals for the Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Programme (2026-27), launched by the Hungarian Government for Bachelors, One-Tier Masters (OTM), Masters and PhD studies.
The deadline for submitting the complete application on both HEC scholarship portal and Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship portal is:
15 JANUARY 2026, at 16:00 HRS (PAKISTAN STANDARD TIME)
SCHOLARSHIP TYPES
Stipendium Hungaricum scholarships are available for Pakistani students fulfilling basic eligibility criteria in specified disciplines and other requirements of Hungarian universities under the following programs:
i. Bachelor's Degree (200 Seats - All available disciplines)
ii. One-Tier Master's (OTM) Degree (70 Seats)
- 20 Slots for Medicine/General Medicine
- 10 Slots for Dentistry
- 40 slots for remaining disciplines
iii. Master's Degree (100 Seats - All available disciplines)
iv. PhD Degree (30 Seats All available disciplines)
