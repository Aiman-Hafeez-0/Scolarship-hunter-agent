ACCREDITATIONS AND RECOGNITIONS

Accreditation confirms that a university's programs meet standards evaluated and verified by independent, subject-specific bodies. IBA maintains accreditation across multiple disciplines, with each program evaluated separately by its relevant accrediting body. These recognitions reflect ongoing evaluation against defined academic and professional benchmarks, not a one-time achievement.
