Graduate Assistantship Schemes

MS Degree Programs: Minimum 70% marks in the GIKI General Admission Test/(GRE/HAT), and 60% marks in the GIKI Subject Admission Test.

PhD Degree Programs: Minimum 70% marks in the GIKI General Admission Test/(GRE/HAT), and 60% marks in the GIKI Subject Admission Test.

Day Scholar Scheme

MS Degree Programs: Minimum 50% marks in the GIKI General Admission Test/(GRE/HAT), and 60% marks in the GIKI Subject Admission Test.

PhD Degree Programs: Minimum 60% marks in the GIKI General Admission Test/(GRE/HAT), and 60% marks in the GIKI Subject Admission Test.

INTERVIEWS

Candidates who qualify the General and Subject Admission Tests as above will be called to appear in the Interview.

TEST/INTERVIEWS CENTERS

The Admission Tests will be held in (Islamabad, Peshawar, Lahore, Quetta and Karachi).

Interviews will be held Online or in Physical mode at GIK Institute

Intra-disciplinary Programs

Candidates are required to apply for admission in a discipline aligned with their previous academic qualification and basic degree program, however prospective students may pursue interdisciplinary admissions by meeting specific criteria. These include demonstrating a strong interest in an interdisciplinary program, completing 6-9 credit hours of deficiency courses, and obtaining approval from the admission committee in addition to fulfilling the institute’s admission requirements. The applicant should pass the GRE Subject/equivalent test with a minimum of 50% marks in the discipline of admission.

Transfer with Advanced Standing

A maximum of 6 credit hours (for PhD) and 9 credit hours (for MS) graduate work done at some PEC accredited and HEC recognized Institution may be transferred with the recommendation of the faculty, provided that;

The work fits into a logical program for the degree

The transfer is approved by the office of graduate studies

A minimum of grade B has been earned.
